Your course plan

After the patch test we recommend the right course and you can choose how to pay.

What we'll ask about

  • Your skin type and how it reacts to sun
  • Current medication, including antibiotics, acne treatments and anything that makes skin sensitive to light
  • Skin conditions, recent tattoos, scars or cold sores in the area
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Recent tanning, sunbeds or fake tan
  • How you've removed hair recently (waxing, threading, epilating)

Before your patch test

  • Shave the area 24 hours before. Don't wax, pluck or epilate for 4–6 weeks before.
  • Avoid sun, sunbeds and fake tan on the area for 2–4 weeks.
  • Come with clean skin: no creams, deodorant or make-up on the area.
  • Tell us about any new medication, even if you've had laser before.

Your information

Your answers include health information, so we treat them with extra care. They are used only to decide whether laser treatment is safe for you, are stored securely by our form provider in the EU/UK, are seen only by your therapist, and are never shared for marketing. You can ask us to see or delete your information at any time. See our privacy notice.
