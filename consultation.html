What we ask

  • Your skin type and how it reacts to sun
  • Medication, including antibiotics, acne treatments and anything that makes skin light-sensitive
  • Skin conditions, recent tattoos, scars or cold sores in the area
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Recent tanning, sunbeds or fake tan

Before your test

  • Shave the area 24 hours before. No waxing, plucking or epilating for 4–6 weeks.
  • Avoid sun, sunbeds and fake tan on the area for 2–4 weeks.
  • Come with clean skin: no creams, deodorant or make-up on the area.
  • Tell us about any new medication, even if you've had laser before.
Your information. Your answers include health information, so we treat them with extra care. They are used only to decide whether laser treatment is safe for you, stored securely by our form provider, seen only by your therapist and never used for marketing.
