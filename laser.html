Laser FAQ

How many sessions will I need?

Most people need a course of 6–10 sessions, spaced several weeks apart, because hair grows in cycles. We'll recommend a course at your consultation.

Does it hurt?

Most clients describe it as a quick snapping or warm sensation. Sensitivity varies by area and we adjust settings to keep you comfortable.

Can I shave between sessions?

Yes, shaving is fine. Please don't wax, pluck, thread or use epilators during your course, as laser needs the hair root in place.

Is laser suitable for everyone?

Not always. Some medications, skin conditions, pregnancy and recent sun exposure can mean we need to wait or adjust treatment. That's what the consultation and patch test are for.

How long is my course valid?

Packs of 6 and 8 sessions are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase; packs of 10 sessions are valid for 18 months.
