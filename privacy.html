Who we are

What we collect and why

  • Bookings are handled by Treatwell under their own privacy policy.
  • Laser consultation form: your contact details and health information, used only to assess whether treatment is safe for you (legal basis: your explicit consent).
  • Payments are processed by our payment providers (Stripe, Klarna, SumUp). We never see or store your full card details.
  • WhatsApp messages are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

How long we keep it

Consultation and treatment records are kept while you are a client and for a limited period afterwards to meet insurance requirements, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, at any time. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
