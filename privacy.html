What we collect and why

  • Bookings are handled by Treatwell under their own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Consultation and treatment records are kept for as long as you are a client and for a limited period afterwards to meet insurance requirements, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, at any time. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
