Good to know

Appointments

Book online any time, or message us on WhatsApp Business. If you need to cancel or reschedule, please let us know as early as you can so we can offer your slot to someone else.

Paying for laser

Book the free patch test online first. Emilia then books your course with you directly, and you can pay by card in the salon or online by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Klarna.

Course validity

Packs of 6 and 8 sessions are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase; packs of 10 sessions are valid for 18 months.
